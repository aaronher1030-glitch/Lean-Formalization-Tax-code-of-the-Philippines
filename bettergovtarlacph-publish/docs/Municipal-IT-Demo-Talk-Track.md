# Better Concepcion demo: municipal IT talk track

Use this as narration while the 67-second, captioned demo video plays. The video has no voice track.

## 0:00–0:05 — Introduction

This is an independent prototype of a public information finder for Concepcion, Tarlac. It is a review artifact, not an official municipal service.

## 0:05–0:12 — Landing page

The opening screen makes the purpose and independent status clear, gives visitors a route to the official municipal website, and introduces the directory.

## 0:12–0:20 — Search

Visitors can search the curated entries by words such as an ordinance, a service, or a publisher. Search operates only on the directory in this prototype. It does not take applications, payments, or personal information.

## 0:20–0:28 — Topics

The entries can be grouped into services, local policies, offices, and procurement. The sample contains seven entries. It is not a complete inventory of municipal information.

## 0:28–0:36 — Original sources

Each entry names its publisher, shows source or checked dates where available, and links to the publisher’s page. The prototype does not copy or certify the underlying record. The LGU would need to confirm source accuracy, canonical locations, and update responsibilities.

## 0:36–0:44 — Status and scope

The About section says that this is an early demo, lists its limited coverage, and does not claim an official partnership. This matters if the prototype is shown publicly or considered for a pilot.

## 0:44–0:51 — Caution

Visitors are directed to verify a record with its original publisher. The prototype is not an official advice channel, transaction system, or guarantee that a document archive is complete or current.

## 0:51–1:00 — Questions for municipal IT

The first scoping conversation should identify the authoritative sources, the person or office responsible for each category, and the rules for updates and corrections. IT can also advise on domain ownership, hosting, security, privacy, accessibility, and records-management requirements.

## 1:00–1:07 — Next step

Agree on a small set of records and validate them with their responsible offices. Then decide whether to revise this independent prototype or prepare an LGU-owned pilot through the municipality’s normal review and approval process.

## Presenter note

The video is a captioned visual walkthrough made from captures of the actual prototype interface. It explains the search and filter behavior but does not show a cursor performing those interactions. Do not describe the demo as an official LGU product or claim that its sample directory has been fully validated.
