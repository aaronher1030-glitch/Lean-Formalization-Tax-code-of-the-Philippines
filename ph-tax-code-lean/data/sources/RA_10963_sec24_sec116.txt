> **Section 38**. Section 116 of the NIRC, as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:
> 
> > "Sec. 116. _Tax on Persons Exempt from Value-added Tax (VAT)._ - Any person whose sales or receipts are exempt under Section 109(BB) of this Code from the payment of value-added tax and who is not a VAT-registered person shall pay a tax equivalent to three percent (3%) of his gross quarterly sales or receipts: _Provided,_ That cooperatives, and beginning January 1, 2019, self-employed and professionals with total annual gross sales and/or gross receipts not exceeding Five hundred thousand pesos (₱500,000) shall be exempt from the three percent (3%) gross receipts tax herein imposed."
