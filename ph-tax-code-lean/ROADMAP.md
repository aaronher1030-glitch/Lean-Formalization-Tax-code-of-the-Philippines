# Roadmap

Where the project stands and what comes next. This mirrors Table 13 of the
paper (`paper/Verified_Core_Tax_Governance_PH.md`, Section 9) and adds the
repo-level follow-ups.

## Remaining work, in order of value

1. **One signature.** A Philippine tax practitioner (or tax lawyer) works
   through the 12-item review packet and signs S1–S9 and S12. This is the
   single highest-value step: every theorem is conditional on the
   specification being a faithful reading of the law, and no practitioner
   has attested to that reading yet. Nothing in the repo can substitute for
   it.
2. **A real-assessment benchmark.** Ground truth from BIR-processed returns
   or published assessments, replacing model-grounded expectations. Requires
   data access the project does not currently have.
3. **Replication across model families.** The three LLM engines are sessions
   of one assistant family. Run the same certificate task on other model
   families and report acceptance and error rates per family.
4. **Harden the specification.** Implement the designed guards for the
   loophole gaps (L1 income realism, L2 election timing, L5 prescriptive
   period), re-prove soundness with the new premises, and mutation-test each
   new guard. Changing the checkers is a versioned specification change, not
   a patch; it needs its own approval and review.
5. **Deductions and the corporate schedule.** The next statutory surface
   where the certificate pattern pays off; also the largest scope increase.
6. **Quarterly mechanics of the 8% election.** Timing, irrevocability within
   the year, and credit mechanics — currently an annual `Bool` flag (the
   unguarded cost of which is demonstrated in LOOPHOLE_FINDINGS.md L2).
7. **Continuous integration.** A CI workflow running `lean/run_demo.sh` and
   the Python harness scripts on every push (the scripts already support
   a `LEAN` environment override for any toolchain location).

## Non-goals

- Formalizing all 292 NIRC sections. Breadth is the companion US project's
  approach; this repo's claim is depth on a small, fully proven core.
- Legal advice, or any claim that the encoded specification is authoritative.
- Replacing BIR systems; the artifact is a research demonstration of the
  certificate pattern.
